Organisation

Reporting lines: who reports to whom. Supervisors inherit access to their team's projects.

A supervisor can access the projects of everyone below them, with at least the same rights. Placing someone above a manager, directly or not, gives them manager rights.

* Manager rights inherited because this person is placed above a manager.
